# Admin Guide — Sani Ul Website

A simple guide for managing your website content.

---

## How to Login

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: **yourwebsite.com/admin**
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your password
5. Click **Sign In**

You will see the admin dashboard.

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows you a quick overview:
- How many gallery images you have
- How many businesses are listed
- How many experience entries exist
- How many blog posts are published
- How many unread messages you have

---

## How to Edit the Homepage

1. Click **Homepage** in the sidebar
2. You will see sections for:
   - **Hero** — The main headline and description
   - **Credibility** — The quick facts shown below the hero
   - **Introduction** — The "About" section on the homepage
   - **Journey** — The timeline section heading
   - **Businesses** — The businesses section heading
   - **CTA** — The call-to-action at the bottom
3. Edit any text you want to change
4. Click **Save Changes**
5. You will see "Changes saved successfully"

---

## How to Edit the About Page

1. Click **About** in the sidebar
2. Edit the sections:
   - **Hero** — Page heading and introduction text
   - **Portrait** — Image URL (from Cloudinary)
   - **Professional Identity** — Your professional description
   - **Philosophy** — Your business philosophy quote
   - **Leadership** — Add or remove bullet points
   - **Personal** — Personal section content
3. Click **Save Changes**

---

## How to Add a Business

1. Click **Businesses** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Business**
3. Fill in the form:
   - Business name
   - Your role
   - Industry
   - Short description
   - Full description
   - Location
   - Website (if available)
   - Responsibilities (add multiple)
4. Toggle **Featured** if this should be highlighted
5. Toggle **Published** to make it visible on the website
6. Click **Save**

---

## How to Edit a Business

1. Click **Businesses** in the sidebar
2. Find the business you want to edit
3. Click **Edit**
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Save**

---

## How to Delete a Business

1. Click **Businesses** in the sidebar
2. Find the business you want to delete
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The business is permanently removed

---

## How to Add Experience

1. Click **Experience** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Experience**
3. Fill in the form:
   - Role/Title
   - Organization name
   - Time period (e.g., "2020 — Present")
   - Check "Current" if this is your current role
   - Type (Current, Past, or Entrepreneurial)
   - Description
   - Responsibilities (add multiple)
4. Toggle **Published** to make it visible
5. Click **Save**

---

## How to Reorder Items

For businesses and experience:

1. Go to the list page
2. Use the **↑** and **↓** buttons to move items up or down
3. Changes save automatically

---

## How to Upload Gallery Images

1. Click **Gallery** in the sidebar
2. Click **Upload Image**
3. Select an image from your computer (JPG, PNG, or WebP)
4. Wait for the upload to complete
5. The image will appear in the gallery
6. Click on an image to edit its title, caption, and category
7. Toggle **Published** to show/hide the image

---

## How to Delete a Gallery Image

1. Click **Gallery** in the sidebar
2. Find the image you want to delete
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The image is removed from the website AND from Cloudinary storage

---

## How to Write a Blog Post

1. Click **Blog** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Post**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — The article title
   - **URL Slug** — Auto-generated from title (you can edit it)
   - **Excerpt** — Short summary
   - **Content** — The full article text
   - **Category** — e.g., "Business", "Operations", "Entrepreneurship"
   - **Tags** — Comma-separated tags
   - **SEO Title** — For search engines
   - **SEO Description** — For search engines
4. Click **Save Draft** to save without publishing
5. Click **Publish** to make it live on the website

---

## How to Unpublish a Blog Post

1. Click **Blog** in the sidebar
2. Find the post you want to unpublish
3. Click **Edit**
4. Click **Unpublish**
5. The post is removed from the public website but kept as a draft

---

## How to Improve SEO (Search Engine Visibility)

Your website already has built-in SEO features. Here is how to make sure search engines find your website:

### Step 1: Submit to Google Search Console

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add property"
3. Enter your website URL: https://saniul.com
4. Verify ownership (choose HTML tag method — add the tag your developer gives you)
5. Once verified, click "Sitemap" in the left menu
6. Enter: https://saniul.com/sitemap.xml
7. Click "Submit"

### Step 2: Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Add your website URL
3. Submit your sitemap: https://saniul.com/sitemap.xml

### Step 3: Create an OG Image

When someone shares your website on social media (Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter), a preview image is shown. To set this:

1. Create a 1200x630 pixel image with your name and title
2. Upload it to your website (via Gallery or any image upload)
3. Copy the image URL
4. Go to Site Settings → SEO tab
5. Paste the image URL in the "OG Image" field
6. Save changes

### Step 4: Keep Content Updated

Search engines rank websites higher when they are regularly updated:

- Add new blog posts regularly
- Update your experience when it changes
- Add new businesses as you start them
- Upload new gallery photos
- Keep contact information current

### Step 5: Use Good Keywords

When writing blog posts or descriptions, use words people might search for:

- "Sani Ul"
- "businessman Bangladesh"
- "entrepreneur Bangladesh"
- "Ayan Trading House"
- "operations manager Bangladesh"
- Your business names and industries

### What Is Already Done (Automatic)

Your website automatically includes:

- **Person Schema** — Helps Google understand this is a person's website
- **Website Schema** — Helps search engines understand the site structure
- **Open Graph tags** — Makes shared links look good on social media
- **Twitter cards** — Makes shared links look good on Twitter/X
- **Sitemap** — Tells search engines about all your pages
- **Robots.txt** — Guides search engine crawlers
- **Semantic HTML** — Clean, readable code for search engines
- **Mobile responsive** — Works on all devices (Google prioritizes mobile)
- **Fast loading** — Optimized for performance

## How to Upload Images

All image fields in the admin panel now use direct upload:

1. Click the upload area (dashed border box)
2. Select an image from your computer
3. Wait for the upload to complete
4. The image appears in the preview
5. To change: hover over the image and click "Replace"
6. To remove: hover over the image and click "Remove"

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF (max 10MB)**

## How to Logout

1. Click your email address in the top right corner
2. Or click **Logout** in the sidebar

---

## Tips

- **Always save after making changes** — Click the Save button
- **Preview changes** — Visit your website in another tab to see how it looks
- **Published vs Draft** — Only published content appears on the public website
- **Images** — Click the upload area to select an image from your computer. It uploads directly to Cloudinary automatically.
- **Backup** — Keep copies of important content before making big changes
